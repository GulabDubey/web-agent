Production (recommended)
-For production, don't use python run.py. Start Uvicorn (or Gunicorn + Uvicorn workers) directly:
>uvicorn app.main:app --host 0.0.0.0 --port 8000
or with multiple workers:
>uvicorn app.main:app --host 0.0.0.0 --port 8000 --workers 4
Note: reload=True and workers>1 cannot be used together. reload=True is for development only.
