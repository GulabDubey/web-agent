What it handles
## The whole club office, in software
Student affairs runs on approvals, calendars and headcounts. Each of those gets one place to live.
  * ### Event scheduling
Publish once and it reaches the campus calendar, the app and every member of the club.
  * ### Approval workflows
New clubs, events and join requests route to the right coordinator and wait in one queue.
  * ### Member directory
One record per student across every club they belong to, with roles and attendance.
  * ### Push notifications
Confirmations, reminders and announcements land on the phone, not in an inbox.
  * ### Activity reporting
Registrations, turnout and club growth per term — the numbers your annual report needs.
  * ### Paid registrations
Collect fees for ticketed events and reconcile them against the club that ran them.


How it runs
## One event, four people, no email
Each step hands off to the next inside the same system. Track where a request is sitting instead of asking who has it.
  1. 01
Coordinator
### Set up the club
Create it, assign a president, pick a category. It appears in the app the moment it is approved.
  2. 02
Club president
### Publish an event
Date, venue, capacity, who can join. It reaches the calendar and notifies every member.
  3. 03
Student
### Register in the app
Browse, open the event, register. Confirmation and reminders arrive on the phone.
  4. 04
Coordinator
### Report on the term
Turnout and club activity are already counted when the annual review comes around.

Questions
## Before you ask
The things campuses check first. Anything else, talk to us.
  * How does BYDO help manage student clubs?
BYDO provides a centralized dashboard where college staff and club presidents can manage member directories, schedule events, track budgets, and streamline approval workflows for all student organizations.
  * Can students discover and join clubs through the app?
Yes, students can browse all available clubs, view upcoming events, and join clubs directly through the BYDO mobile app with instant notifications for club activities.
  * Does BYDO support budget tracking for clubs?
Absolutely. BYDO allows clubs to set budgets, track expenses, and generate financial reports. University admins can oversee budget allocations and approve funding requests.
  * How are club events managed and promoted?
Club presidents can create events with details, set RSVP limits, and send notifications to members. Events appear on the campus calendar for easy discovery by all students.
  * What roles and permissions does the system support?
BYDO supports multiple roles including University Admin, College Staff, Club President, Club Manager, and Student Member — each with customizable permissions and access levels.
