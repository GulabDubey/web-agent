##  Questions? We’ve Got You Covered.
Check out our FAQs to learn how our ERP makes HR management effortless.
1
### How does the Super Admin and Agency model work?
The Super Admin sits at the top of the ecosystem — they create product categories, manage the master catalog, and onboard multiple agencies. Each agency receives stock from the Super Admin and operates independently with their own warehouses, staff, and distribution. Customers then purchase from nearby agencies through the mobile app.
2
### What product categories are supported?
BYDO supports unlimited product categories — Grocery, Machines, Hardware, Bikes, Cars, Electronics, and any custom category your business needs. The Super Admin can create and configure categories to match their exact industry and product lines.
3
### How does the customer app find nearby agencies?
The customer mobile app uses location-based discovery to show products available from agencies in the customer's area. Customers can browse by category, compare options, place orders, and track delivery — all from agencies closest to them.
4
### Can we customize workflows for our business?
Absolutely. Every aspect of the ecosystem is configurable — from product categories and pricing rules to distribution workflows and fulfillment processes. Whether you run a grocery chain, vehicle dealership network, or hardware distribution, BYDO adapts to your operations.
5
### What setup and support does BYDO provide?
BYDO provides end-to-end deployment including initial setup, configuration, data migration, and team training. After launch, you get ongoing technical support, regular updates, and a dedicated team to ensure your ecosystem runs smoothly.
