Frequently Asked Questions
1
### What is BYDO Clinic?
BYDO Clinic is a comprehensive healthcare management platform that connects patients, clinics, doctors, and ambulance services. It offers appointment booking, emergency alerts, real-time ambulance tracking, and powerful dashboards for healthcare providers.
2
### How does the Emergency Alert System work?
When a patient triggers an SOS alert from the mobile app, our system instantly locates the nearest available ambulance, checks bed and ventilator availability at nearby hospitals, and dispatches help — all within seconds. Real-time GPS tracking keeps the patient informed throughout.
3
### Can patients book appointments by specialty?
Yes. Patients can search for doctors by specialty (cardiology, dermatology, orthopedics, etc.), view profiles with ratings and experience, check real-time slot availability, and book appointments instantly from the app.
4
### What dashboards are available for clinics?
Clinics get a comprehensive dashboard with patient queue management, bed occupancy tracking, ventilator availability monitoring, revenue analytics, and appointment scheduling — all in real-time.
5
### Is there a mobile app for patients?
Yes. The BYDO Clinic patient app is available on both Google Play Store and Apple App Store. It offers one-tap emergency SOS, doctor search, appointment booking, health records access, and real-time ambulance tracking.
6
### How do ambulance services benefit from the platform?
Ambulance services receive real-time dispatch alerts, AI-powered route optimization to the nearest hospital, GPS tracking, fleet management, and availability status updates — enabling faster response times and better patient outcomes.
7
### Is the platform available in my city?
BYDO Clinic is expanding rapidly across major cities. Contact our team for availability in your area or to express interest in bringing the platform to your city.
8
### How secure is patient data on the platform?
We take data security very seriously. All patient data is encrypted, access is role-based, and we comply with healthcare data protection regulations. Only authorized healthcare providers can access patient records.
