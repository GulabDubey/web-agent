## What is Smart Shield?
BYDO Smart Shield is a cutting-edge NFC-based attendance system designed for modern smart campuses. It allows students and staff to mark attendance quickly and effortlessly by tapping their NFC cards on the secure Smart Shield device. This seamless system works across classrooms, buses, and staff entry points, ensuring precise and reliable attendance tracking in every scenario. Each tap is instantly recorded and synchronized in real time with the BYDO Campus ERP, eliminating manual entry errors and saving administrative time. With automated monitoring, live updates, and centralized data access, Smart Shield streamlines campus operations while enhancing transparency and security. It provides accurate insights into attendance patterns, boosts student and staff safety, and supports faster, data-driven decision-making for administrators. By integrating advanced technology with intuitive design, Smart Shield empowers educational institutions to manage attendance efficiently, reduce administrative overhead, and maintain complete confidence in the accuracy and integrity of their attendance records.
## How It Works?
Simply tap an NFC card to record attendance instantly, syncing securely across classrooms, buses, staff, and ERP systems
### Tap NFC Card
Students or staff simply tap their NFC card on the Smart Shield device to securely register attendance instantly, without manual effort or delays.
### Instant Verification
The Smart Shield device securely verifies user identity in real time, ensuring accurate, tamper-proof attendance with complete data protection.
### Auto Attendance Marked
Attendance is automatically recorded for classrooms, buses, and staff entries, ensuring accurate tracking across all campus activities in real time.
### Live ERP Update
All attendance data from the Smart Shield device is immediately synchronized with BYDO Campus ERP, ensuring real-time updates across the platform for students and staff.
