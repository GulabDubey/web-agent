### Statutory & Tax Audit
Every company in India must get a statutory audit under the Companies Act. Additionally, if turnover crosses prescribed limits, a tax audit under Section 44AB of the Income Tax Act is mandatory. We conduct both with minimal disruption.
#### Audit Deliverables
Statutory Audit Report — under Companies Act 2013
Form 3CD Tax Audit Report — under Section 44AB
CARO 2020 Report — Companies Auditor's Report Order
Internal Controls Assessment — ICFR review for listed/large companies
Audit Clearance Certificate — For HQ consolidation purposes
#### Process
Pre-Audit Review
Books reviewed and cleaned before audit commences
Audit Execution
Fieldwork, sample testing, management queries
Report Sign-off
Signed audit report issued before Oct 31 deadline

### FEMA & RBI Compliance
Foreign-invested companies face additional reporting obligations under FEMA 1999 and RBI regulations — FLA return, FC-GPR on share allotment, FC-TRS on share transfers, and ODI filings. Missing RBI deadlines can block future remittances.
#### FEMA Filings
FLA Return — Annual Foreign Liabilities & Assets (Jul 15)
FC-GPR — Filed within 30 days of share allotment to foreigners
FC-TRS — On transfer of shares between residents & non-residents
ECB Returns — For external commercial borrowings
Repatriation Advisory — Dividend, royalty, management fee structuring
#### Advisory
FDI Compliance
Sector-specific FDI limits, automatic vs government route
Repatriation Planning
Tax-efficient profit repatriation to parent company
Compounding Applications
RBI compounding for historical FEMA contraventions
Annual + Advisory
### Transfer Pricing
Indian subsidiaries transacting with their foreign parent must comply with Transfer Pricing regulations — arm's length pricing, Form 3CEB certification, and TP documentation. Indian TP regulations are among the most stringent globally.
#### Annual Deliverables
Form 3CEB — Accountant's report on international transactions (Oct 31)
TP Study Report — Benchmarking analysis, comparables selection
Master File — For entities meeting the prescribed threshold
Country-by-Country Report — For qualifying MNC groups
#### Advisory Services
Advance Pricing Agreement
APA filing and negotiation with CBDT
Intercompany Agreements
Drafting and review of service agreements, license agreements
TP Dispute Resolution
MAP and safe harbour analysis
