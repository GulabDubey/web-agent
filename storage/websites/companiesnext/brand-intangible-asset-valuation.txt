Intangible assets like brands, trademarks, intellectual property, and technology play a crucial role in shaping a company’s market value and competitive advantage. At **CompaniesNext** , we help you understand and assess the real worth of these non-physical assets through structured, insightful, and defensible valuations.
Our valuations uphold the tests of integrity, accuracy, and global best practices — enabling our clients to make informed decisions backed by deep domain knowledge and robust analysis.
## What Are Intangible Assets?
In today’s dynamic business environment, intangible assets hold a crucial role. In simple terms, intangible assets can be defined as assets that hold income producing potential but do not have a physical form. Some examples include: :
  * **Brand value and Trademarks**
  * **Customer Relationships**
  * **Patents and Technology**
  * **Licenses, Contracts, and Rights**
  * **Software, Databases, and Proprietary Platforms**


Ongoing the rapid digital transformation and tech-led business models, companies are investing more in intangibles — making their valuation essential for strategic, financial, and regulatory purposes.
## Our Intangible Valuation Services
We offer valuation services for a wide range of intangible assets and related business scenarios:
  * Brand & IP Valuation

  * Purchase Price Allocation (PPA)
  * Licensing & Franchising Valuation
  * Transfer Pricing & Taxation Valuation
  * Impairment Testing (IND AS / IFRS)
  * Internal Business Assessments


## Need for Intangible Asset Valuation
Valuation of intangible assets is critical in several strategic contexts:
  * Mergers, acquisitions, and business combinations as they lead to creation of goodwill which triggers the intangible valuation.
  * Investor reporting as certain regulations require goodwill to be assessed periodically and fund-raising activities.
  * Accounting and financial disclosures.
  * Royalty, licensing, and franchising arrangements.
  * Dispute resolution and litigation support.
  * Transfer pricing and international tax compliance.
  * Internal business planning and performance measurement


## Types of Intangible Assets We Value
  * **Marketing-related Assets**


Brands, trademarks, trade names, domain names, non-compete agreements
  * **Customer-related Assets**


Customer lists, contracts, order backlogs, loyalty data
  * **Contract-based Assets**


Licensing agreements, service or supply contracts, leases, permits, and franchise rights
  * **Technology-based Assets**


Patents, software, proprietary technologies, databases
  * **Artistic-related Assets**


Copyrights, content libraries, music, film rights, and creative IP, artwork
Each category has its own valuation methodology based on usage, control, legal rights, and expected future benefits.
