How quickly can I get an appointment? +
Most experts have same-day or next-day availability. Once you submit a booking, our team confirms your slot within 30 minutes via SMS and email.
Are the experts certified and verified? +
Yes. All Chartered Accountants are ICAI members, Company Secretaries hold ICSI certification, Valuation Experts are SEBI/IBBI registered, and Lawyers are Bar Council enrolled advocates. We verify credentials before onboarding any professional.
Is my information kept confidential? +
Absolutely. All sessions are conducted on an encrypted platform and every expert signs a confidentiality agreement. Your financial and business data is never shared with third parties.
What happens if I'm not satisfied with the session? +
We offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you feel your query wasn't resolved, contact us within 24 hours and we'll either re-match you with a different expert or provide a full refund.
Can I reschedule or cancel my booking? +
Yes, you can reschedule or cancel up to 2 hours before your scheduled session at no charge. Cancellations after that point are subject to a 50% fee.
Do you provide services for Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)? +
Yes. We have experts specialising in NRI taxation, FEMA compliance, cross-border structuring, and overseas investments. Sessions can be scheduled across time zones.
