## Frequently Asked Questions
##  What is a business valuation? 
A business valuation is an in-depth review of your business to establish its current value by considering financial information, the latest market conditions, and industry standards.
##  Why do I need my business to be valued with CompaniesNext? 
CompaniesNext provides a factual and in-depth practical analysis of your business with a guarantee that the whole valuation process is private and confidential — based on proven methods, for the purpose of managing acquisitions, sales, or compliance.
##  What kind of business can CompaniesNext value? 
We value startups, SMEs, companies, and family firms across numerous industries.
##  How long does a valuation take? 
It's generally a process of 5–10 working days, depending on the size and complexity of your business and access to necessary data.
##  What are the methods of valuation CompaniesNext uses? 
We employ multiple methods — Income Approach, Market Approach, and Asset-Based Approach — to achieve a comprehensive and well-rounded value.
##  Can I use the report for legal or tax purposes? 
Yes, our appraisals are comprehensive and suitable for tax filings, legal actions, mergers and acquisitions, as well as financing.
##  What is the cost of a business valuation? 
Prices differ depending on the size and complexity of your business. Contact CompaniesNext to get a personalised quote.
##  What documents will I need to supply? 
We usually need financial statements, tax returns, business plans, and descriptions of assets and liabilities.
##  Is the valuation report kept confidential? 
Absolutely. Your confidential business information is handled under strict confidentiality agreements.
##  How do I initiate a business valuation at CompaniesNext? 
Just get in touch with our team through the website, or give us a call, and we'll take you through the initial consultation and way forward.
