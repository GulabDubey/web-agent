# Due Diligence
Due diligence, in simple terms, is an audit or investigation of a potential business transaction. It is a precautionary process that reduces the risk involved in a transaction by collecting and analysing material facts pertaining to that transaction. We, at Companies Next, can help you with pre and post deal due diligence, either from buy side or sell side. Our team will provide a thorough analysis of data and recommendation to assist you in forming your business decisions.

## **Due Diligence Services**
In an acquisition process, understanding of historical data of ‘Target Company’ is equally important as the potential for future growth of the business is. A comprehensive and in-depth analysis of business help in price negotiation, mitigation of risk associated, and ensure a higher return on investment.  
  
Our experts help you into pre and post-deal due diligence, either from the buy side or sell side. Our team will provide a thorough analysis of data and recommendations to assist you in forming your business decisions. We address a range of financial, commercial, accounting, and operational issues as part of our due diligence process.  
  
**As part of due diligence, we cover the followings:**  
  
**Financial due diligence:** As part of financial due diligence, the financial position of the Target Company is focused and includes analysis of earning history, cash flows, future earning potential, working capital requirements, hidden assets, and liabilities, etc.  
  
**Commercial Due Diligence:** More than financials assessment of target it includes forecast validation, assessment of operational stability and market dynamics in which the company operates.  
  
**Operational Due Diligence:** Our operational due diligence process focuses on the functional and operational effectiveness of the Target Company. It includes assessment of management team, relationship with key stakeholders, distribution network, customer relationships, human resource management, IT system, etc.  
  
**Tax Due Diligence:** The main objective of tax due diligence is to determine the past, present, and future tax liabilities of the Target Company, including disclosed, undisclosed, realized and unrealized tax liabilities.  
  
**OUR OBJECTIVE:**
  * enhancing the buyers understanding of the target business and therefore increasing the chances of successful implementation of the deal and achievement of desired objective; 
  * helping you in taking a right and sound decision through identifying and understanding critical issues that work out as a success factor in a proposed business transaction;
  * highlighting strengths that can be built upon or weaknesses that can be resolved;
  * identifying core strengths that could be used to cover drawbacks that inherently exists in the organization
